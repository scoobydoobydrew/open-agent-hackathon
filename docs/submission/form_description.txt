An AI that can prove it is right, and prove when no answer exists.

THE PROBLEM
Radiation kills most tumour cells, but some survive. Switching off the right genes can push those cells to self-destruct, yet there are millions of possible combinations and every wrong guess is a wasted lab experiment. Chatbots will happily suggest answers. We tested one on 8 cases: 91% of its gene suggestions were wrong, and when no solution existed it invented one.

OUR IDEA: PROPOSE, PROVE, EXPLAIN
1. The AI proposes which genes to switch off.
2. A mathematical referee tests every proposal on a published model of the cell (35 genes) and answers "works" or "fails, because this part of the cell stays alive".
3. Each failure points to what to try next, so the search either finds every smallest solution or proves that none exists. "Impossible" becomes a proven fact, not a guess.
4. Every explanation is split into small steps and each step is re-checked against the cell's rules, so the AI cannot make things up.
Result: never wrong, 34x fewer checks than trying everything, and answers anyone can follow.

BUILT ON THE SPONSORS
- Zetaris: the data layer. Cell rules, scenarios and drug evidence are SQL tables; the AI writes its own safe, read-only queries.
- Meterless Nightshift: every AI request goes through it to NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning 30B, and the app and logs show which model answered each time.
- Meterless H-MEM (official engine): the agent's memory. It keeps verified results and reuses them; tests pass, conformance 13/13.
